# rclone Mac Backup

This page belongs to a personal OAuth client named "Mac Backup rclone".

The owner uses this client with [rclone](https://rclone.org) and [restic](https://restic.net) to make encrypted backups of one personal Mac to the owner's own Google Drive.

The client is for the owner only. It is not a public service and other people cannot sign up for it.
